Adaptive Sampling for Storage of Progressive Images on DNA
X. Pic, N. Pinnamaneni, R. Appuswamy
 BioRxiv / IEEE
Year: 2024 | EURECOM
 Hierarchical wavelet progressive image compression paired with an adaptive oligonucleotide physical sampling policy. Demonstrates multi-resolution visual reconstruction at lower sequencing depth; avoids full-read sequencing costs for visual preview. Gap: Tailored exclusively to 2D image matrices.
HelixVault Impact: Generalizes progressive multi-tier decoding across arbitrary file formats (documents, audio, video).
2
DNA-MGC+: A Versatile Codec for Reliable and Resource-Efficient Storage
R. Khabbaz, J. Mateos, M. Antonini, S. Kas Hanna
 Nature Comp. Sci.
Year: 2025 | CNRS / Pearcode
 Modified Goldman rotating ternary codec (MGC+) targeting simultaneous insertion, deletion, and substitution (IDS) channel noise. Achieves 28% higher logical density over baseline Goldman codes; successfully validated in vitro with Illumina and Oxford Nanopore. Gap: High algorithmic complexity for large batch files.
HelixVault Impact: Implements optimized Base-3 rotating ternary tables with strict homopolymer suppression (≤3 bp) and 40–60% GC tuning.
3
Error-Correcting Codes for Two Bursts of (t1, t2)-DI with Low Complexity
Y. Liu, T. M. Duman (IEEE Fellow)
 IEEE / SNIA
Year: 2026 | Bilkent University
 Construction of linear-complexity channel error-correcting codes tailored for multi-burst deletion and insertion occurrences in sequencing. Provides sub-logarithmic redundancy overhead while guaranteeing exact packet alignment and synchronization under localized burst drops. Gap: Theoretical formulation without software UI.
HelixVault Impact: Implements packetized oligo headers with Reed-Solomon parity to counter burst synthesis-dropout anomalies.
4
Error-Correcting Codes for the Sum Channel
L. Abboud, E. Yaakobi
 ResearchGate / ISIT
Year: 2026 | Technion
 Sum channel matrix algebraic modeling with parity-sum rows for distributed matrix-encoded storage pools. Constructs two-deletion-correcting matrix codes with optimal redundancy bounds 2⌈log2 log2 n⌉ + log2 ℓ. Gap: Focuses on matrix channel abstractions.
HelixVault Impact: Employs horizontal/vertical parity rows to reconstruct missing oligo fragments without re-synthesizing the whole pool.
5
Expected Recovery Time in DNA-based Distributed Storage Systems
A. Levy, R. Con, E. Yaakobi, H. M. Kiah
 RSC Advances / IEEE
Year: 2026 | Technion / NTU
 Erasure-coded multi-container biological architecture with statistical sampling distribution analysis. Derives exact mathematical expectations for random read sampling needed to rebuild damaged or decayed storage containers. Gap: Lacks automated physical lab protocols.
HelixVault Impact: Combines multi-carrier BioDatabase indexing with dynamic freezer S.O.P. generation (Rack/Box/Tube assignments).
6
On the Capacity of Noisy Frequency-based Channels
Y. Gerzon, I. Shomorony, N. Weinberger
 IEEE Access
Year: 2025 | Technion / UIUC
 Short-molecule order-free DNA encoding using concentration histograms rather than ordered base sequences. Bypasses synthesis length barriers by storing data in relative molecular concentrations; proves non-asymptotic capacity bounds. Gap: Susceptible to chemical pipetting noise.
HelixVault Impact: Adopts ordered nucleotide oligos with in-silico PCR primer indexing for high-precision Illumina/Nanopore pipelines.
7
Random Access Expectation in DNA Storage and Fountain Codes
C. Hofmeister, R. Bitar, E. Yaakobi
 Precedence / ITW
Year: 2025 | TU Munich
 Equivalence mapping between binary fully symmetric linear codes and rateless Luby Transform (LT) Fountain codes. Minimizes sequencing read overhead for targeted document queries; provides superior random access without decoding entire pools. Gap: Evaluated primarily through synthetic simulations.
HelixVault Impact: Incorporates Fountain parity health checks and BioCompute direct sequence searching without prior binary decoding.
8
Robust Composite DNA Storage under Sampling Randomness & IDS Errors
B. Tegin, T. M. Duman
 Advanced Science
Year: 2024 | CentraleSupélec
 Composite nucleotide mixtures mapped onto a 3D probability simplex with multinomial channel error-correction. Achieves higher per-cycle density than standard 4-base alphabets while maintaining resilience against deletion and substitution noise. Gap: Requires specialized non-standard synthesis hardware.
HelixVault Impact: Utilizes standard A,C,G,T chemistry compatible with commercial DNA synthesis providers (Twist, IDT, GenScript).
9
The DNA Coverage Depth Problem: Duality & Weight Distributions
M. Bertuzzo, A. Ravagnani, E. Yaakobi
 Nat. Sci. Rev.
Year: 2024 | Eindhoven / Technion
 Combinatorial duality and linear code weight distributions to calculate coverage depth bounds in random sequencing read draws. Provides closed-form formulas for expected read coverage needed to guarantee full-rank matrix reconstruction in small finite fields. Gap: Mathematical proof without practical tool integration.
HelixVault Impact: Calibrates minimum coverage depth thresholds directly in the HelixVault Decoder recovery pipeline.
10
Two Multi-Draw Coupon Collector Models with Retention Rules
A. V. Doumas, S. Spektor
 DNA Alliance / Appl. Prob.
Year: 2026 | Alliance Standard
 Multi-draw coupon collector asymptotics modeling strand dropout probabilities during repeated PCR and enzymatic amplification. Formulates exact variance Var ~ (π²/6)(N²/d²) for molecular recovery; establishes non-uniform dropout rates in physical pools. Gap: Focused strictly on probability theory.
HelixVault Impact: Drives the environmental degradation simulator and Reed-Solomon oversampling rates (≥20%) in HelixVault synthesis.
