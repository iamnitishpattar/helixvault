On the Capacity of Noisy Frequency-based Channels
Y. Gerzon, I. Shomorony, N. Weinberger
IEEE Access • 2025 • Technion & UIUC
 Short-molecule order-free DNA encoding using concentration histograms rather than ordered base sequences. Bypasses synthesis length barriers by storing data in relative molecular concentrations; proves non-asymptotic capacity bounds.

Random Access Expectation in DNA Storage and Fountain Codes
C. Hofmeister, R. Bitar, E. Yaakobi
Precedence / IEEE ITW • 2025 • Technical University of Munich
 Equivalence mapping between binary fully symmetric linear codes and rateless Luby Transform (LT) Fountain codes. Minimizes sequencing read overhead for targeted document queries; provides superior random access without decoding entire pools.

Robust Composite DNA Storage under Sampling Randomness & IDS Errors
B. Tegin, T. M. Duman
Advanced Science • 2024 • CentraleSupelec & Bilkent
 Composite nucleotide mixtures mapped onto a 3D probability simplex with multinomial channel error-correction. Achieves higher per-cycle density than standard 4-base alphabets while maintaining resilience against deletion and substitution noise.
GAP Requires specialized non-standard synthesis hardware.

The DNA Coverage Depth Problem: Duality & Weight Distributions
M. Bertuzzo, A. Ravagnani, E. Yaakobi
Nat. Sci. Rev. • 2024 • Eindhoven & Technion
 Combinatorial duality and linear code weight distributions to calculate coverage depth bounds in random sequencing read draws. Provides closed-form formulas for expected read coverage needed to guarantee full-rank matrix reconstruction in small finite fields.
GAP Mathematical proof without practical tool integration.

DNA Alliance / Appl. Prob. • 2026 • Alliance Standard
