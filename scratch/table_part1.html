R. Khabbaz, J. Mateos, M. Antonini, S. Kas Hanna
Nature Comp. Sci. • 2025 • CNRS / Pearcode
 Modified Goldman rotating ternary codec (MGC+) targeting simultaneous insertion, deletion, and substitution (IDS) channel noise. Achieves 28% higher logical density over baseline Goldman codes; validated in vitro with Illumina and Oxford Nanopore.

Y. Liu, T. M. Duman (IEEE Fellow)
IEEE / SNIA • 2026 • Bilkent University
 Construction of linear-complexity channel error-correcting codes tailored for multi-burst deletion and insertion occurrences. Provides sub-logarithmic redundancy overhead while guaranteeing exact packet alignment and synchronization under burst drops.

Error-Correcting Codes for the Sum Channel
L. Abboud, E. Yaakobi
ResearchGate / ISIT • 2026 • Technion - Israel Inst. of Tech.
 Sum channel matrix algebraic modeling with parity-sum rows for distributed matrix-encoded storage pools. Constructs two-deletion-correcting matrix codes with optimal redundancy bounds 2*ceil(log2 log2 n) + log2 l.
GAP Focuses strictly on matrix channel abstractions.
HELIXVAULT Employs horizontal/vertical parity rows to reconstruct missing oligo fragments without re-synthesizing the whole pool.
